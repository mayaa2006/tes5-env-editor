# The Cartographer's Debt

A four-part questline around Riverwood and Whiterun. An old cartographer's maps are stolen, her apprentice is ambushed, and the man behind it turns out to be an Imperial collector who wants what the maps lead to.

## Characters

- **Hesta Coldwater**: a Nord cartographer in her fifties. Grey braids, ink-stained fingers, a scar across her chin from a fall in a barrow. She rents the corner room at the Sleeping Giant Inn in Riverwood and works at the table by the fire. Sharp and proud, warms to the player slowly. She can't be killed.
- **Rulnik**: Hesta's apprentice, a young Breton man, thin and eager, talks too much. He camps by the river just outside Riverwood, by his campfire, because Orgnar complained about his snoring. Not a fighter: he carries a dagger and hides when there's trouble. He can be killed.
- **Varro Tullius**: an Imperial collector of old things, well dressed in fine clothes, silver-tongued and cold. He rents a room at the Bannered Mare in Whiterun. He only does business after dark. He carries the key to his strongbox.
- **Gerda Stone-Arm**: a Nord mercenary in steel armour, hired by Varro. Big, scarred, practical: she works for coin, not loyalty. A good fighter with a two-handed sword.
- Existing people who take part: Orgnar (the Sleeping Giant's innkeeper), Lucan Valerius (Riverwood Trader) and Hulda (the Bannered Mare).

## Quest 1: Lines on a Map

The quest starts when the player reads a notice pinned to the bar of the Sleeping Giant Inn. The notice says:

> WANTED: A sure-footed guide with a strong stomach. Good pay for a short job. Ask for Hesta Coldwater, corner table by the fire.

Hesta's map case was stolen three nights ago. The thieves fled into Embershard Mine and tore the case apart; three Torn Map Pages are scattered in the mine (one by the forge, one in a chest, one on a table near the back). The player has to find all three pages. Once they have all three, the objective changes to returning to Hesta. Hesta pays 150 gold, or 250 if the player persuades her (Speech 40).

When the player takes the job, Hesta asks them to promise not to tell anyone about the maps. The player can promise or refuse; the promise matters later in the questline.

While the quest is running, Lucan Valerius at the Riverwood Trader mentions a well-dressed Imperial asking about Hesta.

Journal: "I found a notice in the Sleeping Giant Inn asking for a guide. Hesta Coldwater wants to see whoever answers it." When the job is taken: "Hesta Coldwater's maps were stolen and the thieves fled into Embershard Mine. Three pages of her work are somewhere inside." When the player has all three: "I've found all three of Hesta's map pages. I should take them back to her." At the end: "Hesta has her pages back, and paid me for my trouble."

Dialogue with Hesta:

HESTA: You read the notice. Good. Most folk use it to wipe ale off the bar.
  PLAYER: What's the job?
    HESTA: Three nights ago someone broke into my room and took my map case. Twenty years of work.
    HESTA: The thieves ran for Embershard Mine. I followed them as far as the entrance. I'm a cartographer, not a sellsword.
      PLAYER: I'll get your maps back.
        HESTA: The case will be ruined, but it's the pages that matter. There were three of them. Bring me all three, and you'll have 150 septims. (the player takes the job)
      PLAYER: (Persuade, Speech 40) 150 septims for a mine full of bandits? Make it worth my while.
        HESTA: (success) Ha. Fine. 250, and not a coin more. Now go, before I change my mind. (the player takes the job, for 250)
        HESTA: (failure) 150 is what I have. Take it or leave it.
      PLAYER: What's so special about these maps?
        HESTA: They show places that aren't on any map. Barrows the Jarls have forgotten. Roads the Empire never finished.
        HESTA: In the wrong hands they're a grave robber's ledger.
          PLAYER: And in the right hands?
            HESTA: In mine. Where they belong.
          PLAYER: I'll get them back.
            HESTA: Three pages. All three, mind. (the player takes the job)
      PLAYER: Not my problem.
        HESTA: Then stop wasting my firelight. (end the conversation)
  PLAYER: Who are you?
    HESTA: Hesta Coldwater. I draw the maps your Jarls buy and never read.
    HESTA: If you're not here about the notice, I'm busy.

Right after the player takes the job, Hesta asks:

HESTA: One more thing. Nobody hears about these maps. Not the innkeeper, not the guards, nobody. Promise me.
  PLAYER: You have my word.
    HESTA: Good. I'll hold you to it. (she remembers the promise)
  PLAYER: I don't make promises to strangers.
    HESTA: Then keep your mouth shut out of good sense, if not honour.

While the player is looking for the pages:

PLAYER: Where's Embershard Mine again?
  HESTA: East along the road, then up into the hills on the left. You'll hear the bandits before you see them.

When the player comes back with all three pages:

PLAYER: I found your map pages.
  HESTA: All three? Let me see. Yes... yes, this is my hand. The ink's run on the last one, but I can fix that.
  HESTA: Here, as agreed. (she takes the three pages and pays 150 gold, or 250 if persuaded; the quest is done)
  HESTA: Come and see me again in a day or two. I may have more work, if you can keep your feet under you.

Hesta's greetings: while the job is open, "The mine is east of here. Mind the bandits." After the quest: "My pages are drying by the fire. Don't touch them."

Lucan, while the quest is running:

PLAYER: Seen anything strange lately?
  LUCAN: An Imperial in fine clothes came in yesterday asking about "the map woman". Didn't buy anything, either.

Objectives: "Find Hesta's three map pages" (marker on Embershard Mine), then "Return the pages to Hesta".

## Quest 2: The Apprentice's Road

Starts as soon as Quest 1 is finished. Hesta wants a fresh rubbing of the carvings on the Guardian Stones before the autumn rains wear them smooth. Her apprentice Rulnik can do it, but after the theft he won't go alone.

The player meets Rulnik at his camp by the river and escorts him to the Guardian Stones; he follows the player from the moment they agree to go. When they reach the stones, four bandits ambush them: they appear around the stones as the player arrives. Kill all four. If Rulnik dies before the quest is finished, the quest fails ("Rulnik is dead. I've failed Hesta.").

After the fight, Rulnik notices the bandits were paid in new Imperial coin. He stops following the player and walks back to the Sleeping Giant Inn on his own. The player reports to Hesta, who pays 100 gold. When the quest ends, Hesta and Rulnik talk at the inn (a short scene).

Hesta, to start it:

HESTA: Back on your feet? Good. I have work, and this time I need you to keep someone alive.
  PLAYER: Who?
    HESTA: My apprentice, Rulnik. He's camped by the river, the fool, because Orgnar complained about his snoring.
    HESTA: The Guardian Stones need a fresh rubbing before the rains wear the carvings smooth. Rulnik can do it, but after the theft he won't go alone.
      PLAYER: I'll take him.
        HESTA: Good. Bring him back in one piece. He's an idiot, but he's my idiot. (the player agrees; objective: meet Rulnik at his camp)
      PLAYER: Why do the Stones matter?
        HESTA: Because whoever stole my maps knew exactly what they wanted. The Stones are on two of those pages.
          PLAYER: I'll take him.
            HESTA: Mind the road. It's not as quiet as it was. (the player agrees)
      PLAYER: What's in it for me?
        HESTA: A hundred septims, and Rulnik's eternal gratitude, which is worth considerably less.
          PLAYER: Fine, I'll take him.
            HESTA: Good. He's by the river. Follow the sound of complaining. (the player agrees)

Rulnik, at his camp, once Hesta has sent the player:

PLAYER: Hesta sent me.
  RULNIK: Oh, thank the Divines. I've been sitting here jumping at every rabbit.
  RULNIK: Is it true what she says? That you went into Embershard alone?
    PLAYER: It's true.
      RULNIK: Then I'm in good hands. Lead the way. I'll be right behind you. Probably closer than you'd like. (Rulnik starts following; objective: take Rulnik to the Guardian Stones)
    PLAYER: I had help.
      RULNIK: Help's good. I like help. Lead on, then. (Rulnik starts following)
    PLAYER: Let's just go.
      RULNIK: Right. Yes. Going. (Rulnik starts following)

Rulnik, after the bandits are dead:

PLAYER: Are you hurt?
  RULNIK: I don't think so. I hid behind the Warrior Stone. Very brave of me.
  RULNIK: One of them had a purse full of new Imperial coin. Bandits don't get paid in new coin.
    PLAYER: Someone hired them.
      RULNIK: That's what I thought. Hesta needs to know. I'll finish the rubbing and head back to the inn. Tell her I'm right behind you. (objective: tell Hesta what happened)
    PLAYER: Just finish the rubbing.
      RULNIK: Yes. Right. Then I'm going straight back to the inn. You tell Hesta. (objective: tell Hesta what happened)

Hesta, when the player reports:

PLAYER: Rulnik's safe. And someone paid those bandits.
  HESTA: Paid them? In new Imperial coin?
  HESTA: There's only one Imperial who's been asking about me. Lucan told me about him.
  HESTA: Thank you. Here's your hundred. Keep your eyes open. (she pays 100 gold; the quest is done)

The scene at the inn when the quest ends:

SCENE:
HESTA: You let them get close enough to bleed on you.
RULNIK: I let them get close enough to bleed on the rubbing. It's fine. Mostly.
HESTA: Mostly. Sit down before you fall down.

Objectives: "Meet Rulnik at his camp", "Take Rulnik to the Guardian Stones", "Kill the bandits", "Tell Hesta what happened".

## Quest 3: Business After Dark

Starts as soon as Quest 2 is finished. The Imperial is Varro Tullius, staying at the Bannered Mare in Whiterun. Hesta wants proof that he hired the thieves.

Varro only talks business after dark (from 8 in the evening until 4 in the morning). During the day, Hulda tells the player he keeps strange hours.

Varro carries Varro's Strongbox Key. The player has to get it (pickpocket it, or take it from him some other way). His strongbox is in the Bannered Mare, locked, and it holds Varro's Ledger. Reading the ledger moves the quest on. Varro also offers to buy Hesta's maps: if the player agrees, he pays 200 gold now and remembers it (this changes Quest 4). The player can agree and still steal the ledger.

The ledger reads:

> 3rd of Last Seed. 60 septims to Krev and two others, Embershard. One map case. Delivered three pages short. Paid half.
> 9th of Last Seed. 200 septims to the road men. Guardian Stones. Watch the apprentice. Discourage him.
> Hearthfire. The Coldwater woman's maps show the way into Volskygge's inner vault. The buyer in Solitude will pay four thousand.

The player brings the ledger to Hesta. She keeps it and needs a day to copy it, and tells the player to come back tomorrow. A day later she has news: Varro left Whiterun at dawn with a mercenary. The quest ends when the player talks to her the next day.

Hulda, during the day:

- **Player:** "I'm looking for an Imperial called Varro."
  - **Hulda:** "The Imperial? He keeps strange hours. Comes down after dark and drinks alone in the corner."

Varro, after dark only:

**Varro:** "Ah. You have the look of someone Hesta Coldwater would hire. Sit. Drink?"
- **Player:** "I know you hired those thieves."
  - **Varro:** "Know is a strong word. Suspect, perhaps. Suspicion is cheap. I deal in things that are worth something."
    - **Player:** "Like Hesta's maps."
      - **Varro:** "Like Hesta's maps. Twenty years of roads and ruins, and she keeps them in a case a child could open."
      - **Varro:** "I'll pay you well to bring me those pages. She need never know who sold them."
        - **Player:** "Deal."
          - **Varro:** "Sensible. Two hundred now, the rest when I have the pages. Don't keep me waiting." *(he gives the player 200 gold and remembers the deal)*
        - **Player:** "Hesta's maps aren't for sale."
          - **Varro:** "Everything is for sale. Only the price changes. Good night."
        - **Player:** "[Persuade, Speech 50] Tell me who else you've hired, and I'll think about it."
          - **Varro (success):** "A few lads from the Embershard crew, and some road trash for the Stones. Cheap help. I see now I get what I pay for."
          - **Varro (failure):** "Nice try. Think about my offer."
- **Player:** "Who are you?"
  - **Varro:** "Varro Tullius. A collector of old things. Old maps, old debts. I pay well for both."
- **Player** (only if the player promised Hesta in Quest 1): "Hesta told me nothing, if that's what you're fishing for."
  - **Varro:** "Loyal. How refreshing. And how expensive, for you."

Varro, during the day: "Not now. Come back when the sun's gone."

Hesta, when the player brings the ledger:

- **Player:** "I found Varro's ledger."
  - **Hesta:** "Give it here. Gods. He paid them in coin and wrote it down like a grocer's bill." *(she takes the ledger)*
  - **Hesta** (only if the player promised in Quest 1): "And you kept your word, too. I won't forget that."
  - **Hesta:** "I need to copy this before the Jarl's steward sees it. Give me a day. Come back tomorrow."

A day later:

- **Player:** "Is the copy ready?"
  - **Hesta:** "It is. And Varro's gone. He left the Bannered Mare at dawn with a mercenary. Rulnik saw them take the road towards Valtheim Towers."
  - **Hesta:** "Rest. You'll need it." *(the quest is done)*

Objectives: "Find proof that Varro hired the thieves" (marker on Varro), "Bring Varro's ledger to Hesta", "Come back to Hesta tomorrow".

## Quest 4: Settling Accounts

Starts as soon as Quest 3 is finished. Varro has left the Bannered Mare and made camp beside the road below Valtheim Towers, with Gerda Stone-Arm guarding him. Hesta asks the player to end it, one way or another.

Gerda stands between the player and Varro. The player can pay her 300 gold to walk away (she leaves for good), talk past her, or fight. Varro can be persuaded (Speech 75) to leave Skyrim, in which case he walks away and is gone; or he and Gerda (if she's still there) attack and the player kills Varro. If the player took Varro's money in Quest 3, Varro greets them as a partner instead, and when he learns there are no pages he attacks.

There are two endings, each with its own journal entry: Varro left Skyrim, or Varro is dead. Either way the player goes back to Hesta. She pays 300 gold and gives the player the Coldwater Survey of the Rift (a book she wrote: "Being a true survey of the roads, barrows and forgotten places of the Rift, made by Hesta Coldwater of Riverwood, with notes on what lies beneath."). If the player took Varro's money, she pays only 100 gold and tells them not to come back, but still gives the survey.

Gerda:

- **Gerda:** "That's close enough. State your business or walk on."
  - **Player:** "I'm here for Varro."
    - **Gerda:** "Then you're here for me first. He pays, I stand in the way. That's the arrangement."
      - **Player** (needs 300 gold): "What if I paid you more?"
        - **Gerda:** "More than Varro? He's tight with coin. Three hundred, and I was never here." *(she takes 300 gold and leaves for good)*
      - **Player:** "Then we fight."
        - **Gerda:** "Suits me." *(Gerda and Varro attack)*
      - **Player:** "Let me talk to him first."
        - **Gerda:** "Talk. But I'm watching your hands."

Varro:

- **Varro** (only if the player took his money in Quest 3): "My partner! You've brought the pages, I hope?"
  - **Player:** "There are no pages. I lied to you."
    - **Varro:** "Then you're as worthless as she is. Gerda!" *(he attacks)*
- **Varro** (otherwise): "Hesta's dog. I wondered when you'd turn up."
  - **Player:** "It's over, Varro. Hesta has your ledger. The Jarl's steward will see it."
    - **Varro:** "The Jarl's steward can be bought. Everyone can be bought."
      - **Player:** "[Persuade, Speech 75] Not this time. Leave Skyrim tonight and nobody hangs."
        - **Varro (success):** "...Fine. Cyrodiil is warmer anyway. Tell the old woman she won. This time." *(he leaves Skyrim; this is the first ending)*
        - **Varro (failure):** "I don't think so. Gerda, deal with this." *(he attacks)*
      - **Player:** "Then I'll do this the old way."
        - **Varro:** "As you like." *(he attacks)*
  - **Player:** "Why do you want her maps so badly?"
    - **Varro:** "Volskygge. Her pages show the way into the inner vault. A buyer in Solitude will pay four thousand septims for what's down there."
    - **Varro:** "You could have a share. A large one."
      - **Player:** "Not interested."
        - **Varro:** "Pity."

Killing Varro is the second ending.

Hesta, afterwards:

- **Player:** "It's done. Varro won't trouble you again."
  - **Hesta** (if Varro left): "Gone back to Cyrodiil with his tail between his legs. Better than he deserved."
  - **Hesta** (if Varro is dead): "Dead, then. I won't pretend to weep."
  - **Hesta** (if the player took Varro's money): "His ledger had a new entry. Two hundred septims, to you. Here's what I owe you, and the survey. Don't come back." *(she pays 100 gold and gives the survey)*
  - **Hesta** (otherwise): "Here. Three hundred, and something better: the first copy of my survey. Your name's in the margins." *(she pays 300 gold and gives the survey)*

Epilogue greetings: Hesta, "The survey's selling better than I hoped. Don't let it go to your head." Rulnik (at the inn), "I'm taking the next rubbing myself. Well. With you. If you're free."

Objectives: "Find Varro's camp below Valtheim Towers", "Deal with Varro", "Return to Hesta".
