QUEST: A Drink for the Mare

SCENE 1 — The Bannered Mare, Whiterun. Hulda behind the bar.

HULDA: The Honningbrew cask was due three days ago. Three days!
  PLAYER: I can fetch it for you.
    HULDA: Would you? Sabjorn at the meadery owes me one cask, paid in full. Tell him Hulda sent you. (the player takes the job)
  PLAYER: Not my problem.
    HULDA: Then drink something else.

SCENE 2 — Honningbrew Meadery. Sabjorn is fretting.

PLAYER: Hulda sent me for her cask.
  SABJORN: Her cask. Yes. Well. There's a small problem. Skeevers in the cellar, and the cask's down there with them.
    PLAYER: I'll clear them out.
      SABJORN: You will? Then the cask's yours to carry. (the player agrees to clear the cellar)
    PLAYER: (Persuade, Speech 40) Hulda paid you. Bring it up yourself.
      SABJORN: (success) Fine, fine. Here. Don't drop it. (he gives the player the cask)
      SABJORN: (failure) Not with those things down there.

SCENE 3 — The cellar. Five skeevers. The cask of Honningbrew Mead sits by the stairs.

When the skeevers are dead:

PLAYER: The cellar's clear.
  SABJORN: Shor's bones. Take the cask, then. (he gives the player the cask)

SCENE 4 — Back at the Bannered Mare.

PLAYER: One cask of Honningbrew.
  HULDA: Finally! Here, for your trouble. (she takes the cask and pays 50 gold; the quest is done)
