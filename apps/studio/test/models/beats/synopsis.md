# The Missing Shipment

- Quest giver: Gerdur, who owns the lumber mill in Riverwood.
- Gerdur's shipment of lumber to Whiterun never arrived. She asks the player to find out what happened.
- The player finds the wrecked cart on the road just north of Riverwood. A note lies in the mud next to it: "Take the wood to the camp by the Guardian Stones. —R."
- At the Guardian Stones there's a bandit camp: three bandits and their leader, Ragnvald.
- The player can kill them all, or talk Ragnvald down (Speech 35): he hands over Gerdur's ledger and leaves.
- If Ragnvald is killed, the player takes Gerdur's ledger from his body.
- Bring the ledger back to Gerdur. She pays 200 gold, or only 100 if Ragnvald was let go, because the wood is lost for good.
