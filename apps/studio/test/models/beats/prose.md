# The Lantern by the River

In Riverwood there lives an old woman called Tilda Grey-Hand, in a small house of her own by the river. Every night she lights a lantern in her window for her son, Arn, who went up to Bleak Falls Barrow a year ago and never came back.

If the player asks her about the lantern, she tells them about Arn and begs them to find out what happened to him. Up at Bleak Falls Barrow, just inside the entrance, lies what's left of Arn, and on him a letter he never sent. In it he says he was ashamed to come home with nothing to show for himself.

When the player brings the letter back, Tilda reads it and weeps, then thanks them and gives them her late husband's axe. From then on the lantern stays dark.
