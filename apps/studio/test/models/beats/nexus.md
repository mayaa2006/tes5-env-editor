# The Fallen Watchman (quest mod)

Adds one new quest to Whiterun Hold, about 20 minutes long.

## Features
- New character: Captain Ulfgar, an old guard captain, drinking at the Drunken Huntsman in Whiterun.
- New character: Hrothmar, a deserter turned killer, hiding out at Halted Stream Camp.
- New item: Watchman's Oath, a unique steel sword.

## How it starts
Pick up the bloodied guard's badge lying in the grass outside Whiterun's western watchtower. Your journal tells you to show it to the guard captain at the Drunken Huntsman.

## Walkthrough (spoilers)
1. Show the badge to Captain Ulfgar. He recognises it: it belonged to his nephew, who was found dead on patrol. He asks you to find the killer.
2. Hrothmar is at Halted Stream Camp. You can kill him, or convince him to surrender (Speech 50); if he surrenders he walks to Whiterun and turns himself in.
3. Go back to Ulfgar. Either way he pays you 300 gold and gives you Watchman's Oath.
